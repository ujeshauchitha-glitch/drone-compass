## 2026-10-13 — Gate S1 PASSED; Gate S2 FAILED (study stopped at Stage 2, per PLAN.md section 7)
* **Gate S1 passed**: checks (a)–(f) pass for the verified H16 tensors (and for the recalled set R). Two independent yield implementations (eigenbasis vs Liouville) agree to ~1e-15 on 200 directions. Primary anisotropy ΔΦ = 0.2057 (Φ from 0.3345 to 0.5402), far above the 1e-3 threshold. Richer ASSUMED models dilute it to ≈0.01 (R2, R3); relaxation at 1e7/s reduces it to ≈1.5e-3 (scan-based). See `results/stage1_*.csv`.
* **Gate S2 FAILED as pre-registered** (`results/stage2_gate.json`): K3orth, ε = 1, Poisson only. Gate condition 1: ratios of MC median error to CRB-predicted median error at the three large-N points = 14541, 25858, 45982 (needed 0.5–2). Gate condition 2: log–log slope = −0.033 (needed −0.5 ± 0.1). Thresholds unchanged.
* **Cause (diagnosed, not a coding error)**: the N5/N10 tensors are diagonal in the molecular frame, so the molecule has exact π-rotation symmetries and Φ(±nx, ±ny, ±nz) = Φ(n) (checked numerically: mirror images give identical counts to 4e-13). Any element set whose rotations are axis permutations (K2, K3orth) inherits this, giving a 4-fold *mirror* ambiguity (up to ~90° apart) on top of the polarity ambiguity. The global ML estimator picks a mirror image about half the time (log-likelihood of the mirror image is equal to within noise), so MC medians are dominated by this and cannot approach the *local* CRB. The CRB itself is also singular at the three coordinate axes for this set.
* **Exploratory diagnostic (NOT a gate result, added after the failure, labelled as such)**: a fixed generic non-orthogonal K = 3 set (rotation vectors (0.3, 0.5, 0.2) and (−0.4, 0.2, 0.6) rad) removes the ambiguity: ratios 0.934, 0.898, 0.893, slope −0.483.
* Unit test `test_mle_recovers_truth_near_crb` was changed from a single K3orth assertion to two cases: K3orth is `xfail(strict=True)` documenting the ambiguity, K3generic must pass. This changes a unit test, not a pre-registered gate threshold or gate configuration.
* **Decision needed from the study owner before continuing**: either (A) amend PLAN.md with a clearly labelled post-hoc amendment (Gate S2 evaluated on the optimised/generic K = 3 set, K3orth reported as a failed baseline and a core negative finding), or (B) stop here and report. Nothing past Stage 2 has been run.
